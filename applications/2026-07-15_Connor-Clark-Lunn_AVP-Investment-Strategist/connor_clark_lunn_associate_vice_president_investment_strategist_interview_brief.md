**2. How do you analyze portfolio exposures - duration, yield curve, credit, sector?**
At Moody's I analyze duration positioning, yield-curve sensitivity, and credit/cross-asset exposures, aggregating security-level positions to portfolio-level metrics. I stress under parallel and non-parallel curve moves and cross-check sensitivity consistency at the aggregate level, escalating anything not economically defensible.

Use Story 4 (LDI study shifting a pension fund's SAA): built the scenario analysis, decomposed duration-gap contribution to funding-ratio volatility, presented to the investment committee with explicit SAA recommendations; the committee adopted a duration extension and the client returned for follow-on work.

**4. How do you influence portfolio managers and stakeholders without direct authority?**
Use Story 2 (escalated a defensible-but-wrong output) and Story 3 (bridged client investment team and dev): I lead with decomposed evidence, translate between technical and investment audiences, and frame recommendations around the stakeholder's decision logic - which is why I became a Head of Risk's direct escalation contact.

**5. How do you use data tooling to support recurring investment publications?**
Re-engineered manual spreadsheet workflows into auditable Python pipelines (pandas, NumPy) feeding recurring charts and dashboards; use Plotly/matplotlib for visualization and agentic-AI workflows (Claude Code, Cursor) to accelerate drafting and validation - keeping quarterly-outlook-style deliverables consistent and repeatable.
