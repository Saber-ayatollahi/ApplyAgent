## What You'll Be Doing

The Senior Manager of Treasury Analytics plays an important role in supporting the
bank's management of **consumer behavioural risk**. This position offers the
opportunity to contribute to the Treasury Analytics function and participate in the
execution of key mandates. As Senior Manager, you will help generate strategic
insights, analyze risk drivers within retail products, and ensure that retail risk
modeling is accurately reflected in business and technology processes.

Success depends on advanced critical thinking and the ability to analyze complex
issues with hands-on involvement in analytics development and execution. You will
communicate analytical findings to management and business partners.

## How You'll Succeed

**Systems and Support (Infrastructure)**
- Support implementation of business initiatives; communicate Treasury Analytics
  methodologies and address analytic/data requirements across CIBC teams.
- Maintain the **pricing, risk, and analytics library**, ensuring integration and
  consistency across Treasury systems.
- Develop and execute test plans, QA, and **user-acceptance testing** to validate
  analytics and methodologies.
- Ensure all models and methods are properly **vetted and approved**, supporting
  consistency of risk measures and **model risk management**.

**Financial / Interest Rate Derivative Analytics**
- Support design and implementation of analytics for risk representation and
  **funds transfer pricing (FTP)** of retail products.
- Develop pricing and risk analytics to identify and manage **hedge-able risks**.
- Prototype pricing and hedging strategies for **fixed income derivatives**.
- Apply knowledge of **interest rate derivatives (swaps, swaptions)** valuation and
  market risk concepts to retail banking products.

**Consumer Behavioural and Quantitative Analysis**
- Apply customer behaviour modeling (**GLM, survival analysis**) to support Treasury
  Analytics **parameter reviews**.
- Develop, improve and maintain the analytics library — tools, models and techniques
  for modeling customer behaviour and **interest rate risk**.
- Implement mathematical models, processes and algorithms with operational
  effectiveness, accuracy and thorough documentation.
- Query and derive insights from data using **Python, R, SQL**.

**Management and Enhancement of Cash-Flow Modeling Books (CFMB)**
- Model retail risk representation in the **cashflow modeling book (CFMB)**.
- Collaborate with Lines of Business to understand behavioural changes and evolve
  the program for effective risk modeling and hedging.
- Work with the **Treasury CFO group** to interpret and explain cash-flow modeling
  **P&L variability**.
- Enhance **PnL decomposition** processes; support daily and monthly CFMB P&L.

## Who You Are

- **3+ years** in **Asset Liability Management (ALM)**, analytics, modeling and risk
  management with a bank and/or other financial institution.
- **3+ years** in quantitative analytics — valuation, hedging, risk measurement
  (strong asset). Exposure to valuation and risk metrics of **interest rate
  derivatives** an asset.
- Strong experience in applied mathematical, quantitative, statistical or
  **stochastic modeling**.
- Strong system and quantitative programming: **C++/Python/R/SQL** or high-level
  languages.
- Experience with **retail banking products** — commitments, mortgages, GICs.
- Knowledge of financial economics, financial mathematics (**fixed income theory**),
  statistics, treasury processes, risk management, **balance sheet modeling**, and
  **option modeling** techniques.
- Ability to express modeling insights with an aptitude for explaining **PnL and
  risk drivers**.
- Experience in **advanced GLM (including Cox regression), panel time series and
  model selection**.
- **Master's/Doctoral degree** in mathematics, computer science, quantitative
  finance, engineering, statistics or related technical subject.
