## Top 5 Likely Technical Questions

**1. Walk me through how you'd conduct due diligence on an external manager or fund.**
Draw on the manager-oversight framing from Moody's: assess investment process, performance attribution, risk metrics, and team/organizational stability. Emphasize independent review — I hold delegated sign-off on portfolio outputs and routinely challenge results that are mathematically defensible but economically unsupported (STAR Story 2), then synthesize findings into a memo for senior leadership.

**2. How do you translate macro views into portfolio positioning?**
Use the Ortec LDI study (STAR Story 4): I built a stochastic economic scenario generator calibrated to client assumptions, ran funding-ratio and return distributions under base and stressed regimes, decomposed which exposures drove volatility, and presented explicit allocation recommendations the investment committee adopted. Macro data → scenarios → attribution → actionable positioning.

**3. Explain VaR vs. CVaR and how you've used them in optimization.**
At Ortec I ran asset-only and asset-liability (surplus) optimization on both VaR and CVaR using the GLASS platform, plus risk decomposition, risk budgeting, and near-optimal frontier analysis to test how robust an allocation is to assumption changes. CVaR captures tail severity beyond the VaR threshold — important for alternatives with skewed/fat-tailed payoffs.

**4. How do you use Python in an investment workflow?**
I re-engineered manual spreadsheet workflows into auditable Python pipelines (pandas, NumPy, SciPy) with logging and validation, and use SQL day-to-day. I also built agentic-AI review workflows (Claude Code, Cursor) that cut development cycle time ~30-40% (STAR Story 7) — while keeping a human sign-off on anything governance-critical.

**5. Tell me about coordinating a complex, multi-stakeholder investment process.**
Use the university pension merger (three single-employer plans into a JSPP) on a team of three: developed and validated the model capturing all Funding Policy dynamics and studied duration, currency, inflation, and leverage overlays — or the Calypso→PFaroe migration where I bridged client investment desks and the development org (STAR Story 3).
