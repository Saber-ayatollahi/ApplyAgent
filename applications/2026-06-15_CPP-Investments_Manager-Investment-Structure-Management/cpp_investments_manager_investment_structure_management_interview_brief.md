## The One Competency Gap to Prepare For

**Direct experience setting up and maintaining legal entities (entity governance, jurisdictional filings, board minute books, regulatory compliance for wholly-owned investment entities).** The Master Repo evidences cross-functional structural coordination and governance documentation but not hands-on entity formation/maintenance.
