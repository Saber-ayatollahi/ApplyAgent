### Residual Honest Gaps — Own These in Interview

**Gap 1 — No trade execution experience.**
The JD requires 'execute trades in derivative and cash products.' The repo has zero trading-desk execution evidence. Saber's experience is analytical, advisory, and model-governance. Do not bridge this verbally by saying 'I've been close to execution' — it will not survive a trading-desk interview. Honest framing: 'My background is on the analytics and model side of these instruments; I have not been in a seat-based execution role. What I bring is deep familiarity with how these instruments price and behave, and I am building toward the execution dimension.'

**Gap 2 — No sell-side relationship experience.**
The JD asks candidates to 'develop strong relationships with sell-side trading firms.' The repo has no evidence of sell-side counterparty relationships. Do not claim this. Honest framing: 'My relationships have been on the buy-side and platform side; the sell-side relationship-building is something I would be developing in this role.'

**Gap 3 — Bloomberg not evidenced.**
The JD lists Bloomberg as an asset. The repo has no Bloomberg evidence. If asked, honest answer: 'I have worked with Bloomberg data outputs in an analytical context but have not been a primary terminal user.' Do not list Bloomberg on the resume.

**Gap 4 — Credit positioning not evidenced as a primary skill.**
The JD asks for credit as part of the alpha-generation mandate. The repo supports spread calibration review (Moody's) and general multi-asset scenario analysis, but not credit portfolio construction or credit trading analytics. Do not claim credit positioning. Honest framing: 'Spread dynamics and credit-risk overlays have been part of the scenario and sensitivity work I do, but dedicated credit portfolio construction is not my primary depth.'

**Gap 5 — 'Minimum 5 years of relevant trading and portfolio management experience.'**
Saber has ~7.3 years of finance experience but none in a formal trading or portfolio-management seat. The JD's qualifier is 'trading and portfolio management.' Saber's experience is modelling, advisory, and model governance. This is the central eligibility risk for this application. The resume and cover letter have been drafted to maximise the legitimate overlap (liability hedging, derivatives analytics, portfolio construction from the liability side) without misrepresenting the nature of his roles. The hiring team will make their own judgment; the honest path is to surface this gap if asked rather than obscure it.
