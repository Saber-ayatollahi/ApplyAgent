## Likely technical / case questions

**1. "Walk us through a transformation engagement you delivered end to end."**
Use the EY IFRS 17 story: a Canadian insurer with finance, actuarial, and IT out of sync on data sourcing and CSM mechanics. I built shared requirements documentation, walked each function through the others' constraints, isolated the three decisions needing executive sign-off, and escalated them clearly. The program hit its reporting-readiness milestone and the documentation was reused across subsequent IFRS 17 engagements.

**2. "How do you approach designing or enhancing a risk framework and its governance artifacts?"**
Start from the decision the framework must support, then the evidence chain: methodology, documentation standard, review-and-challenge step, escalation path, and performance monitoring. That is the structure I work within on Moody's model governance committee — methodology review, documentation and benchmarking standards, and model-performance assessment. At Ortec I designed stochastic risk-management frameworks tailored to individual client mandates, so I have built as well as reviewed.

**3. "Tell us about a time you challenged a result under deadline pressure."**
A client delivery run passed every internal check but the portfolio-level sensitivities did not square with the client's economic intuition under one rate shock. I held the release, decomposed sensitivities by asset class, found a curve-calibration edge case at the short end, and escalated to the product owner and the client's Head of Risk with a remediation plan. Release slipped 48 hours; the client avoided acting on wrong numbers and I became their direct escalation contact.

**4. "How have you used AI platforms and agents, and data analytics tools, in delivery?"**
Validation workload was growing faster than headcount, so I built agentic review workflows in Claude Code and Cursor for first-pass code review, validation scaffolding, and documentation drafts — with a human retaining sign-off on anything governance-critical. Cycle time on comparable modules dropped roughly 30–40%. The governance point matters as much as the speed: AI drafts, the reviewer attests.

**5. "How do you run multiple simultaneous engagements of different scope?"**
During the Calypso-to-PFaroe migration I ran concurrent client onboardings while translating investment-desk requirements into structured Product Owner requests. The lever was reusable pattern-making: the configuration pattern built for the first client was reused across the migration cohort, which is how you protect quality when scope multiplies.
