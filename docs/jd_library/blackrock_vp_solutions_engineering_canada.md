## About the team
Aladdin = BlackRock's unified investment operating system (portfolio mgmt, trading, ops, risk; public + private markets). The Solutions Engineering (SE) team uses deep product/markets/workflow expertise to design and present best-in-class Aladdin solutions to prospective clients across asset managers, insurers, pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, and alternative investors. Works with Business Development, Product, and Implementations.

## Responsibilities
- Lead engagements with small/medium prospects and support large prospect engagements from RFP → discovery → solution development → demonstrations → Proofs of Concept (PoCs) → preferred vendor selection.
- Understand prospect objectives and map them to Aladdin solutions using industry terminology, workflows, and pain points.
- Track market movements, regulatory changes, and geopolitical events affecting prospects/products/strategies.
- Deliver tailored demonstrations by client type, industry segment, and requirements.
- Expertise in at least one client segment or product vertical.
- Gather prospect intelligence; identify patterns in client feedback; relay product requirements to internal teams.
- Improve sales processes, engagement models, and team governance.

## Skills & Qualifications
- Strong understanding of the Aladdin platform and front-to-back investment workflows, OR 5 years investment management, risk management, or alternative investments software experience.
- Translate technical concepts into clear business value.
- Outstanding communication and presentation skills (complex → simple, to senior audiences).
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Strong interest in financial markets and investment technology.
