Position Overview
The VP, Portfolio Management, Investing & Oversight is accountable for the end‑to‑end digital experience that enables how portfolios are constructed, managed, monitored, and reviewed across Richardson Wealth and IA Private Wealth.
This role bridges Investments, Advisory, Compliance, Operations, Data, and Technology to deliver a modern, compliant, and scalable investment and portfolio management experience for advisors and clients. The focus is not product management of investment solutions, but rather how investment and portfolio management comes to life digitally across advisor and client journeys. The VP plays a critical leadership role in advancing the Wealth businesses’ digital strategy, improving advisor productivity, strengthening compliance‑by‑design, and elevating the client experience.
This is an existing vacancy.
Duties And Responsibilities
Digital Portfolio Experience Leadership
 Own the digital investment and portfolio management experience across advisor and client channels, from proposal through ongoing portfolio management and review.
 Partner with key stakeholders including the Wealth Management Programs and Portfolio Advisory Services and Advisor Experience teams to define and continuously improve advisor workflows for:
 Portfolio construction and model selection
 IPS creation, alignment and suitability validation
 Rebalancing and ongoing portfolio maintenance
 Performance, risk, and fee transparency
 Ensure the client facing experience clearly communicates portfolio insights, performance, and progress toward goals.
Portfolio & Model Enablement
 Partner with Wealth Management Programs and Portfolio Advisory Services and Advisor Experience teams to digitize model portfolio management, including:
 Model lifecycle governance (approvals, versioning, documentation)
 Change notifications and advisor workflows
 Embedded controls for drift, risk tolerance, and concentration
 Enable scalable portfolio management capabilities such as tax aware rebalancing, householding, and portfolio monitoring.
Compliance, Risk & Governance
 Embed regulatory and suitability requirements directly into digital journeys, ensuring compliance by design across Richardson Wealth and IA Private Wealth.
 Partner closely with Compliance, Risk, and Legal to ensure:
 Auditability of advice and portfolio decisions
 Clear evidence of client acknowledgement and disclosures
 Automated exception reporting and resolution workflows
Data, Insights & Analytics
 Define portfolio analytics and insights delivered digitally, including risk, performance, asset allocation, and portfolio diagnostics.
 Partner with Data & Architecture and other key stakeholder teams to establish standards for benchmarks, security master data, pricing, and performance calculation.
 Leverage data to deliver actionable insights for advisors at the book and household level.
Platform Integration & Operational Efficiency
 Align portfolio management workflows with trading, operations, and custody platforms to improve straight through processing and reduce manual effort.
 Partner with Operations and Technology to:
 Reduce exceptions and NIGO rates
 Improve advice to execution timelines
 Enhance reliability and scalability of portfolio related platforms
Leadership & Stakeholder Management
 Act as a senior thought partner to Wealth leadership on digital investment and portfolio strategy.
 Lead cross functional forums and decision making across Investments, Advisory, Technology, Compliance, Data, and Operations.
 Influence at the Director and VP level without direct authority, aligning diverse stakeholders around shared outcomes.

Profile & Experience
Required Experience

Preferable Experience

 Experience modernizing legacy advisor or investment platforms at scale.
