About the job
About the Role:



The Vice President Investment Research and Portfolio Managerment will play a key leadership role in shaping and executing Equiton Capital's investment strategy while overseeing the continued evolution of the firm's investment platform. This executive role is responsible for driving investment research and analysis, advancing proprietary product development, and ensuring strong governance, performance, and scalability across the platform. Working closely with executive leadership, the VP of Research and Product Risk will help identify new market opportunities, enhance existing offerings, and contribute to the firm's long-term investment direction.


The ideal candidate is a strategic investment leader with deep market expertise, strong product development experience, and the ability to translate investment insights into scalable solutions that support growth and deliver value to investors.


Key Responsibilities:



Investment Strategy & Platform Leadership

Act as a key contributor in shaping the firm’s investment strategy, providing insight and leadership across portfolio construction, market positioning, and long-term growth initiatives.


Support the evolution and enhancement of the firm’s investment platform, including the development and refinement of product offerings aligned with market opportunities and investor demand.


Provide strategic input into investment decisions through analysis, diligence, and portfolio insights, supporting disciplined capital deployment across the platform.


Provide leadership and oversight of the firm's Investment Fund Manager (IFM) and Portfolio Management (PM) activities, ensuring investment processes, governance frameworks, and fiduciary obligations are effectively executed.


Research & Investment Support

Lead and contribute to investment research initiatives to inform decision-making across the firm.
Support underwriting, diligence, and strategic evaluation of new opportunities and partnerships.
Provide internal expertise and perspective to guide investment committees and senior leadership discussions.


Governance, Risk & Compliance Oversight

Oversee key compliance functions as they relate to investment products, fund structures, and regulatory requirements.
Strengthen and provide oversight of risk management frameworks across proprietary products, ensuring alignment with regulatory requirements and the firm’s risk tolerance.
Ensure robust governance frameworks are in place across all investment vehicles and strategies.
Partner with compliance and legal teams to ensure adherence to regulatory obligations and industry best practices.
Support the firm's regulatory obligations as an Investment Fund Manager and Portfolio Manager by ensuring appropriate policies, procedures, controls, and documentation standards are maintained.


Business Growth & Stakeholder Engagement

Act as a senior ambassador for the firm in client, partner, and investor discussions.
Represent the firm at industry events, conferences, and strategic meetings, helping to strengthen market presence and deepen key stakeholder relationships.
Collaborate with sales and relationship teams to enhance the firm’s ability to gain and retain clients.


Operational Excellence

Ensure strong oversight of investment processes, reporting, and performance measurement across the platform.
Contribute to strategic planning, budgeting, and initiatives that support the firm’s growth and scalability.
Contribute to the execution of investment strategies and asset allocation frameworks through analysis and portfolio insights that support performance and client acquisition.
Continuously support the evolution of the investment platform by identifying opportunities that enhance the firm’s value proposition and market positioning.
Deliver forward-looking market insights and portfolio perspectives that support business development, client engagement, and revenue growth.
Oversee portfolio construction and performance with a focus on delivering consistent, compelling outcomes that reinforce client trust and attract new mandates.
Proactively identify risks and opportunities to protect client assets while enhancing long-term portfolio value and client satisfaction.
Lead high-conviction research and investment selection that differentiates the firm’s offering in competitive sales situations.
Translate complex investment insights into clear, client-focused narratives that support sales conversations and decision-making.


What You Bring:

Proven leadership experience managing investment teams and driving performance.
Deep expertise in portfolio management, asset allocation, and risk management.
Strong understanding of capital markets and the ability to translate insights into strategy.
Demonstrated success in investment research and decision-making.
Excellent communication and presentation skills, with confidence in client-facing settings.
Strong client service orientation and relationship management skills.
Ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced, high-performance environment.
High attention to detail with sound judgment and decision-making capabilities
Entrepreneurial mindset with a passion for continuous improvement.
Strong knowledge of regulatory frameworks and a demonstrated commitment to compliance, risk controls, and best practices in portfolio management.
Experience building and operating within Investment Fund Manager (IFM) structures, including developing fund governance frameworks, operational processes, and regulatory compliance programs.
Proven ability to build and enhance institutional investment platforms, including the governance, controls, and operating frameworks required to support scalable Investment Fund Manager (IFM) and Portfolio Manager (PM) operations.


Qualifications:

Minimum of 15 years of relevant investment expertise demonstrated by success in managing, multi-asset class portfolios, including alternative investments for high-net-worth clients as a CIO or Portfolio Manager
CFA designation is required
Must hold current registration as a Portfolio Manager (PM) with the applicable Canadian securities regulatory authority
Direct experience operating within a registered Investment Fund Manager (IFM) and/or Portfolio Manager (PM) environment, including responsibility for fund governance, investment oversight, regulatory compliance, and investment operations.
Bachelor’s degree required, masters preferred (Business, Economics, Accounting, Finance)
